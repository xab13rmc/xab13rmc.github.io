La Maison

Ourpassion.

A gastronomic Maison in Andorra. Mediterranean, family cuisine, carefully chosen produce and a team that works quietly so you only have to receive.

01 — Our passion

A cuisine that comes from the land.

At Maison Beauvert we specialise in exceptional culinary experiences for every kind of event, from intimate celebrations such as birthdays and baptisms to elegant weddings and corporate events.

We are committed to culinary excellence, innovation and support for biodiversity, local producers and the circular economy.

Mediterranean, family cuisine shapes our menu throughout the seasons, a deliberate choice to awaken the senses.

Our values.

The way we work is part of what we serve.

01

Transparency

We speak clearly from the first quote to the last detail of service.

02

Ethics

Working with local produce and suppliers who share our standards.

03

Sustainability

We minimise waste, avoid plastic and consider the impact of every event.

04

Inclusion

Every table should have room for everyone, with options for all diets.

02 — From morning to night

From the first coffee to the last service, the Maison changes with the day.

We follow the rhythm of every celebration: morning light, lunch at midday, aperitif in the afternoon and service as the sun goes down.

03 — Let's talk

Have an idea? We'll set the table.

We explain how we work and design the proposal that fits your event together.
