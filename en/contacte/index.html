Frequently asked questions.

If you can't find the answer, write to us.

What is the minimum number of guests?
It depends on the format and date. Tell us and we will confirm the minimum for your event.
How far in advance should I book?
For weddings and large events, as early as possible. For platters and breakfasts, about a week.
Do you travel outside Andorra?
We work in Andorra and nearby. Ask us about the specific location and we will confirm.
Can I taste the menu beforehand?
Yes. For weddings we organise a personalised tasting to fine-tune every detail.
Do you offer options for diets and allergies?
Yes. Tell us in the form and we will adapt the proposal.
